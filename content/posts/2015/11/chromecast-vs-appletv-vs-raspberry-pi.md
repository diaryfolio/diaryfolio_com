<br />
Let's compare between &nbsp;Chromecast vs AppleTV vs Raspberry Pi vs Roku vs Others in 2015. Previous version comparison <a href="https://diaryfolio.com/2013/07/google-chromecast-vs-raspberry-pi-vs.html" target="_blank">can be found here</a><br />
<h4>
<u><span>Chromecast 2</span></u></h4>
<ul>
<li>Google came with a new design for Chromecast 2, with a much better antenna (802.11ac) and perfect price ($35)</li>
<li>Built in apps like HBO, Spotify, Youtube, Netflix (but all its competitors have these apps)</li>
<li>Though aesthetic is much better, In my opinion it doesn't matter as it sits behind the Television</li>
<li>Much improved casting from your phone, Laptop.. almost any devices with a cast option</li>
<li>Also I liked that it doesn't have a remote, coz you can control from your phone</li>
<li>Lacking power to play games</li>
</ul>

<h4>
<u><span>Apple TV4</span></u></h4>
<ul>
<li>Coming with Top Specs and a cool Remote Control (Though I feel your iPhone is more than sufficient), Apple TV4 released in 2015-September boasts of (Price around $150)</li>
<li>32/64GB storage, 802.11ac WiFi, A8 Processor</li>
<li>USB C (3C) Connectivity but only for service/support</li>
<li>Bluetooth 4.0, IR sensor, HDMI 1.4, Ethernet, Power cable</li>
<li>Remote Control with Siri voice recognition, Bluetooth &amp; IR, &amp; Lightning port for charging</li>
</ul>

<h4>
<u><span>Raspberry Pi 2 (model B)&nbsp;</span></u></h4>
<ul>
<li>Priced around $35 and best suitable if you are a developer. Can be very well used as Full HD entertainment mechanism. <a href="https://www.raspberrypi.org/products/raspberry-pi-2-model-b/" target="_blank">Details here</a></li>
<li>Released in 2015 Feb, 900 Mhz A7 Cortex CPU, 1GB RAM, 4 USB ports</li>
<li>Full HD supported. Can run full Linux or Windows 10 (who really want to run Windows?)</li>
<li>HDMI, MicroSD slot, 800 MA power, Approx 45gms&nbsp;</li>
<li>Sits Quiety and very easy to put an OS and do whatever you want !! &nbsp;Check here to find various media center's/ OS available for RPi2</li>
</ul>
