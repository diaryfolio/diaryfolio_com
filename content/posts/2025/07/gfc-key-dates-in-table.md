<p class="article-lead">A concise timeline of the S&amp;P 500 during the Global Financial Crisis, from the October 2007 peak through the March 2009 low.</p>

<h2>Key dates during the crisis</h2>
<div class="timeline-table"><table class="markdown-table events-table">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Event</th><th scope="col">Approximate date</th><th scope="col">What happened</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Initial peak</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2007-10-09</td><td data-label="What happened">S&amp;P 500 reached 1,565.15, its pre-crisis high.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Bear market begins</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">Late Oct 2007</td><td data-label="What happened">A slow decline began after the peak.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Bear market confirmed</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-01-09 to 2008-03-17</td><td data-label="What happened">The index fell about 20%; the period included the Bear Stearns bailout.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Short-term bottom</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-03-17</td><td data-label="What happened">The index reached roughly 1,256 before a temporary rebound.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Bear-market rally</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-05-19 to 2008-06-05</td><td data-label="What happened">The index rallied to roughly 1,428 but did not recover its prior high.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Major leg down</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-09-15</td><td data-label="What happened">Lehman Brothers collapsed and markets fell rapidly.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Panic-selling bottom</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-11-20</td><td data-label="What happened">The index reached roughly 752, about 52% below the 2007 peak.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Relief rally</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2008-12-08 to 2009-01-06</td><td data-label="What happened">A short-term bounce carried the index to approximately 935.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>Final leg down</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2009-01-06 to 2009-03-09</td><td data-label="What happened">The decline continued to a new cycle low.</td></tr>
<tr><td data-label="Event"><strong>GFC bottom</strong></td><td data-label="Approximate date">2009-03-09</td><td data-label="What happened">Lowest close: 676.53. A long-term recovery began afterward.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
