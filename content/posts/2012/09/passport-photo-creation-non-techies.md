If you believe that you can't follow the <a href="https://diaryfolio.com/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using.html">GIMP tutorial</a>, &nbsp;you can upload your photos and the sites will do the rest so that it will become printable. Please note, you may need to think of privacy as you are uploading photo to a "cloud service"<br />
<div>
<ol>
<li>epassportphoto - Pros:Very flexible, have multiple countries, simple to use. &nbsp;Cons: Generates only 4 photos and rest used for ads</li>
<br />
<li>portraitbooth - &nbsp;Pros: 8 photos in one go &nbsp;Cons: Bit clunky</li>
<br />
<li>idphoto4you - &nbsp;Pros: Flexible, more than 70 countries &nbsp;Cons: file formats limited.</li>
</ol>
<br />
Once you have the photo ready, go to a high street shop like Boots and get a printout. &nbsp;Prefer 6"x4" photos.<br />
<h3>
epassportphoto</h3>
<ul><br />
<li><a href="http://www.epassportphoto.com/" target="_blank">http://www.epassportphoto.com/</a></li>
<br />
<li>Choose particular country (eg. UK) then press button: get my photos.</li>
<br />
<li>Follow instructions. (everywhere put No Thanks !!)</li>
<br />
<li>Use "Download passport photo sheet image"</li>
</ul>
<h3>
portraitbooth</h3>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://uk.portraitbooth.com/" target="_blank">http://uk.portraitbooth.com/</a></li>
<br />
<li>Click button: Start Photo Booth, pops open a new window (make sure your system does not block it poping up)</li>
<br />
<li>Upload your photo, click Browse button, tick box agree to conditions in line 2, click submit button in line 3</li>
<br />
<li>Leave default on 8 photos and don't sign for any other services.</li>
</ul>
