<ol><br/>	<li>Create UKBA account.</li><br/>	<li>Log-on the UKBA site.</li><br/>	<li>Next page , click 'Book Appointment' button.</li><br/>	<li>Question is : 'Which service are you applying for?'  -&gt; Learn them beforehand and answer appropriately.. (eg choose SET(O) for ILR based on employment based categories)</li><br/>	<li>Next screen, 'Are you applying using the premium service?' -&gt; Select YES</li><br/>	<li>Remember to put any dependents with you.</li><br/>	<li>Put the range of dates you wish to apply.  You could select maximum upto 3 PEO's.</li><br/>	<li>It will present the suitable options. Select and Then press NEXT with the suitable date</li><br/>	<li>Confirm.</li><br/>	<li>Ensure you receive Email confirmation</li><br/></ol><br/>[ad#ad-3]<br/><br/>Please also See how to <a href="https://diaryfolio.com/peo-appointment/" target="_blank">get appointment to PEO</a>
