This tutorial is for semi-techies who knows a bit of GIMP (image editing). &nbsp; This tutorial is for UK style passport photo (<b>45mm x 35 mm</b>) which is widely used in UK, Australia, New Zealand, India etc. &nbsp;This is a quick and easy process and one can create&nbsp;Passport photos at home<br />
<br />
<span>If you are non-technical, use<a href="https://diaryfolio.com/2012/09/creating-passport-photos-yourself-at.html" target="_blank"> this link&nbsp;</a>&nbsp;.&nbsp;</span>If you want to create United States (USA) Passport photo or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) photo, please<a href="https://diaryfolio.com/2014/03/create-your-own-united-states-passport.html" target="_blank"> follow this link</a><br />
<h3>
<span>How to Make your own Passport Photo - Prerequisite</span></h3>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://www.gimp.org/downloads/" target="_blank">GIMP</a> - One of the best image editing tools and its completely Free</li>
<li>USB stick or any memory device to store and take to nearby shop</li>
<li>A quality Digital camera</li>
<li>Local Shops where you can print. Normally it costs <b>(£0.15 or 25 US cents) to print 8</b> photos</li>
</ul>
<h3>
<span>Steps (Video Tutorial attached blow of this page)</span></h3>
<ul>
<li>Ask one of your colleague to take a photo &nbsp;of you with a light background. Further <a href="http://epassportphoto.com/Wizard.aspx" target="_blank">details of how to take a photo</a>&nbsp;yourself &nbsp; &nbsp;</li>
<li>&nbsp;<a href="http://s3.amazonaws.com/static.epassportphoto.com/img/img03.jpg"><img alt="" class="alignnone" src="http://s3.amazonaws.com/static.epassportphoto.com/img/img03.jpg" /></a></li>
<li>Take multiple pictures so that you can choose from the best</li>
<li>A sample Photo shown below. Ensure that you have sufficient space above your head and your shoulders are clearly visible.&nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart1-300x195.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart1-300x195.jpg" /></a></div>
</li>
<li>I assume the photo you selected is a high quality image. <b>DO NOT</b> reduce the quality/pixels of the original image. Right click on the image and open with GIMP. (Hope you installed gimp into your machine)&nbsp;</li>
<li>From your gimp "Toolbox", click the "Rectangular Select" tool. (Ensure that the&nbsp;"Fixed Aspect Ratio" is unticked). &nbsp;Draw a small rectangle surrounding your face. In the left hand-pane, choose Size, <strong>Enter width=350 px and height=450 px</strong>. &nbsp;(or a ratio of this) and then <strong>select</strong> "Fixed Aspect Ratio". &nbsp;You will see a rectangle area in the image to be adjusted accordingly. (Please recheck the Aspect ratio is 350 x 450 as sometimes, GIMP will reset it)</li>
<li>Using mouse select a rectangle. Ensure it includes the hair, but fill approx 75% with the face. Feel free to increase your selection area if the image is big, as long as the aspect ratio is 350 x 450.</li>
<li>Now Crop the image and should look like below. &nbsp; from GIMP Menu &nbsp;<strong>Image -&gt; Crop to Selection .&nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart2_cropped1_1.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" height="320" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart2_cropped1_1.jpg" width="248" /></a></div>
<div class="separator">
</div>
</strong></li>
<li>Now we will resize the image. &nbsp;From GIMP menu, <strong>&nbsp;Image -&gt; Scale Image</strong> and select<strong> width=750 px</strong>. The height <strong>should</strong> become 964 px. &nbsp;If its not becoming 964, something went wrong. Start again</li>
<li>Open a new GIMP window (File -&gt; New), and specify <strong>width = 814 px, height = 1092 px</strong>, and specify <strong>"fill with background"&nbsp;</strong>as below . &nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/gimp_new_image.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" height="320" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/gimp_new_image.jpg" width="276" /></a></div>
</li>
<li>This will ensure correct margins are left for the image before duplicating them. Copy (Control-C) and paste(Control-V)&nbsp;into new window. Click on the "white" border surrounding your photo, so that all layers are merged.&nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart3_cropped_1_1.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" height="320" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart3_cropped_1_1.jpg" width="238" /></a></div>
<div class="separator">
</div>
</li>
<li>Now click on the white border. Then Select Filters -&gt;Map -&gt; Tile from GIMP window. &nbsp;"Tile to new size", then &nbsp;disconnect the chain symbol (by clicking on it) and set the&nbsp;<strong>width = 3256 px &amp; height = 2184 px&nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Tile_disconnect-300x217_1.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" height="144" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Tile_disconnect-300x217_1.jpg" width="200" /></a></div>
</strong></li>
<li>Ensure the "Create New Image" box is ticked. Result should have 8 photos (as shown below)&nbsp;<div class="separator">
<a href="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart4_multi-300x201.jpg" imageanchor="1"><img border="0" src="/assets/images/original/2012/09/create-your-own-passport-photo-using/Clipart4_multi-300x201.jpg" /></a></div>
</li>
<li>Save the result as a new file with jpg extension, and with<strong> no-compression</strong> i.e. set to <strong>100%</strong>. Please ensure no compression is done for quality photos.&nbsp;(In newer GIMP2, you can do "File -&gt; Export To &nbsp;", then save it as with jpg extension with "passportTile.jpg")</li>
<li>Save the .jpg file to a <u>memory stick</u>.</li>
<li>My local "BOOTS" shop allows USB memory stick, SD card, CD and many other storage to be used . Select 6x4 inch prints, which should print 8 photos. (Select no margins at boots) &nbsp; or Alternatively you could print from your home printer: &nbsp;From GIMP&nbsp;Image -&gt; Print-size ,&nbsp;width = <strong>6 inches</strong>, height = <strong>4</strong> <strong>inches</strong>. Then print with File -&gt;Print</li>
</ul>

<h3>
Experienced Users</h3>
Just the values only if you are repeating the process<br />
<pre>- UK Passport Photo Aspect Ratio: width = 350 px, height = 450 px.
- Scale to 750px wide.
- New window: width = 814 px, height = 1092 px.
- Tile to: width = 3256 px, height = 2184 px.</pre>
