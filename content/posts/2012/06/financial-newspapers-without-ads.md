I follow quite lot of Business News.  Though the news are so much manipulated, it gives an overall idea of what's going around the business world around you.<br/><ol><br/>	<li> I like Bloomberg's ipad application as its very intuitive.</li><br/>	<li>Financial Times (www.ft.com) is unfortunately paid.</li><br/>	<li>"Economic Times" is good to get news at macro Level, but unfortunately its flooded by Ads !!</li><br/></ol><br/>So you all might know how to read these news papers without ads?<br/><ol><br/>	<li>Bloomberg is decent, so not worth the effort to remove ads</li><br/>	<li>Financial times trick is to use google cache.  For clues on various tags use their RSS  - <a href="http://www.ft.com/rss/home/uk">http://www.ft.com/rss/home/uk</a>  and use the meta information to search in another window of google. Then click on cache to read the article. (eg Search in google for  site:ft.com Mon, 04 June 2012)</li><br/>	<li>Economic times is based in India but they too have RSS feeds which is good.  Use the link <a href="http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/rss.cms">http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/rss.cms</a>  to the news without ads.</li><br/></ol><br/>In general, if you get the link use the keyword "cache:" in google and put the url.  or use link directly after http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:&lt;your_link&gt;<br/><blockquote><em><a href="http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:http://www.ft.com/cms/s/0/43eef134-ae66-11e1-94a7-00144feabdc0.html">http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:http://www.ft.com/cms/s/0/43eef134-ae66-11e1-94a7-00144feabdc0.html</a><br/><br/><br/></em></blockquote>
