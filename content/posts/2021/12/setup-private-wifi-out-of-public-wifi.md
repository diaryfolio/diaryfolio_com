<h2>A word on Privacy</h2><p>While you are travelling, it is essential that you connect your mobile phone or laptop to Public Wifi especially in Airports, Hotels, Cafe's, Malls etc. Remember quite a lot of your personal information is <a href="https://www.techlicious.com/tip/how-to-protect-your-privacy-on-public-wifi-networks/" target="_blank">exposed</a> by such direct connections and is quintessential you isolate your personal device from public Wifi even if you have VPN. It is recommended to turn-off WiFi , BlueTooth during the course of journey but until unless you need it.</p><h2>What are we trying to do</h2><p>So the aim of this article is to ensure that you detach your device from public network as below.&nbsp;</p><div class="separator"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEhOjRM108ybNFpJfJ4Md-ulowEUTQ4ZOkOf18YDy6i-jHlPheYbkmhG9gGx8rTtBo3COwtcXqG_0cLw2vgP8M51UGPFM16V8US4qrajNicPl99nsrTfIeCJ1uJuwCd61I8AW3JdZu5QGPMLOxH9KcyPWq2JeYSCQSETLIs_TVahn8LlTG_TsUmEpOno5Q=s622"><img border="0" data-original-height="622" data-original-width="542" height="400" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEhOjRM108ybNFpJfJ4Md-ulowEUTQ4ZOkOf18YDy6i-jHlPheYbkmhG9gGx8rTtBo3COwtcXqG_0cLw2vgP8M51UGPFM16V8US4qrajNicPl99nsrTfIeCJ1uJuwCd61I8AW3JdZu5QGPMLOxH9KcyPWq2JeYSCQSETLIs_TVahn8LlTG_TsUmEpOno5Q=w349-h400" width="349" /></a></div><p>So using the GL.inet&nbsp;device, we will&nbsp;</p><p></p><ul><li>Accept a Public Network connection (WAN) via wired or wireless</li><li>Apply VPN settings</li><li>Create a separate Private Network (LAN) which your personal devices can connect to</li></ul><p></p><h2>Pre-Reqs</h2><p></p><ul><li>OpenWRT based devices (such as <a href="https://www.amazon.co.uk/GL-iNet-GL-MT300N-V2-Converter-Pre-installed-Performance/dp/B073TSK26W" target="_blank">GL-MT300N</a>, <a href="https://www.amazon.co.uk/GL-iNet-GL-MT1300-Wireless-Pocket-Sized-Repeater/dp/B08MKZXGBY" target="_blank">GL-MT1300 Beryl</a> etc<img border="0" data-original-height="434" data-original-width="1914" height="146" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjRCXM9qwUbVrytoJbxn8GOhgnARZmiZ4Jjo8mbPw975Ut8DVLhghzqnnzGkjaXj8BKpt7Jms8DW89L4XWyaMlBYBaA7dV9n_2MFqzwyojwFHZEXTraTR1ZTY5ngyTUtYls2oAO9RN9h8-nbr6WUrWMHNU2eEjD6vFqqgaiH0W74SwV6AwmaFxh4VmtZw=w640-h146" width="640" /></li><li>LAN cable (for first time setup) is highly recommended</li><li>VPN connection (eg NORDVPN, SurfShark etc).
